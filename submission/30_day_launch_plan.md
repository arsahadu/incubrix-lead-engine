# IncuBrix: 30-Day Lead Generation Launch Plan

## Goal

Run a small, compliant creator-outreach pilot to validate targeting, messaging, and workflow performance before considering a larger campaign.

This plan is a proposal. No live campaign results have been measured yet.

## Week 1 — Validate and prepare

- Confirm IncuBrix's actual product capabilities, target customer, demo, and value proposition.
- Review candidate records and distinguish verified qualified leads from unverified candidates.
- Confirm the public evidence supporting creator identity, recent activity, commercial offer, workflow need, and business contact route.
- Check applicable privacy, marketing, and platform rules for the prospect's country and channel.
- Configure email authentication (SPF, DKIM, and DMARC) if email outreach is approved.
- Test scheduling, tracking, and reply logging using accounts controlled by the team.

**Owner actions:** Confirm the product claims, approve the qualification checklist, review the first prospects, and complete test-account checks.

**Exit condition:** No real prospect is contacted until the contact route, qualification evidence, message, and compliance requirements have been reviewed.

## Week 2 — Run a controlled pilot

- Start with up to 10–20 approved contacts per working day only if legally permitted and operationally appropriate. This is a proposed cap, not a guarantee of safe volume.
- Use appropriate public business contact routes and follow each platform's rules.
- Identify the sender and organization, personalize messages truthfully, and provide a clear opt-out where required.
- Use no more than two respectful follow-ups, subject to channel rules and any applicable legal requirements.
- Record delivery failures, replies, opt-outs, and complaints.
- Test each channel only through accounts owned by the team until its integration is actually demonstrated.

**Owner actions:** Review sending logs and campaign health daily; pause immediately if complaints, platform warnings, or deliverability issues arise.

## Week 3 — Improve targeting and messaging

- Compare results by creator segment, channel, and message version.
- Check whether positive replies come from genuinely qualified prospects.
- Remove invalid contacts, duplicate records, and candidates whose evidence is stale.
- Review personalization for accuracy and avoid unsupported claims about the creator's workflow.
- Pause ineffective or non-compliant channels.

**Owner actions:** Document what the results support and select the next test based on evidence rather than assumptions.

## Week 4 — Evaluate and decide

- Calculate actual delivery, reply, positive-reply, meeting, and opt-out rates.
- Record sample sizes and distinguish observed outcomes from forecasts.
- Decide whether to continue, change targeting, revise the value proposition, or pause the campaign.
- Increase volume only if the pilot supports it and legal, platform, and deliverability checks remain satisfactory.

**Owner actions:** Produce a results summary and a recommendation for the next month.

## Planning scenarios — not measured results

A LinkedIn B2B outreach study from Belkins reported an overall reply rate of 7.2% across more than 15 million outreach touchpoints. Results depend on channel, audience, campaign design, and the denominator used. This benchmark is contextual reference material, not an IncuBrix performance forecast.

For initial planning, use the following assumptions:

| Metric | Conservative scenario | Better-performing scenario |
|---|---:|---:|
| Reply rate per delivered message | 3% | 5% |
| Meeting rate per delivered message | 0.5% | 1% |

These are proposed test assumptions, not established industry averages or proven IncuBrix results. The meeting-rate range is a planning assumption, not a measured estimate derived from the cited LinkedIn study.

### Illustrative outcome for 100 delivered messages

| Outcome | Conservative scenario | Better-performing scenario |
|---|---:|---:|
| Replies | 3 | 5 |
| Meetings | 0.5 expected | 1 expected |

Fractional meetings represent an expected rate across repeated campaigns, not a literal fraction of a meeting. Actual results may be zero or higher.

Do not treat all replies as positive. Track positive replies separately, and only count meetings that are actually booked.

## Metrics and definitions

- **Qualification rate:** Candidates meeting every required criterion divided by candidates assessed.
- **Contact validity rate:** Valid business contact routes divided by contact routes checked.
- **Delivery rate:** Delivered messages divided by attempted messages.
- **Reply rate:** Prospects who reply divided by delivered messages, using a consistent definition throughout.
- **Positive reply rate:** Positive replies divided by delivered messages.
- **Meeting rate:** Meetings booked divided by delivered messages.
- **Opt-out rate:** Opt-outs divided by delivered messages.

Report counts alongside percentages, especially for small samples.

## Guardrails

- Do not treat candidate discovery or automated scoring as proof of qualification.
- Do not send outreach to unverified prospects.
- Do not scrape private data, bypass access controls, or evade platform restrictions.
- Respect privacy requirements, marketing laws, platform rules, and opt-outs.
- Test sending only to accounts controlled by the team until real integrations are explicitly approved and verified.
- Do not claim automated scheduling, tracking, or reply handling works on a live channel unless an end-to-end test demonstrates it.
- Do not report planning assumptions as observed campaign results.

## Current implementation status

The current project contains 1,111 discovered candidate records, a draft-generation workflow, and a local test mode for scheduling records, tracking events, and simulated replies.

**Important limitations:** The candidate records are not 1,111 verified qualified leads. The local test mode demonstrates a limited local workflow, not a fully integrated three-channel outreach engine. No real creator outreach or live campaign performance is claimed.

Before submission, present these as prototype capabilities and clearly list the remaining verification and integration work.
